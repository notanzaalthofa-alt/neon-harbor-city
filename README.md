Neon Harbor is a sun-soaked open-world crime game: drive and steal cars, outrun the police, and run missions across a living coastal city. Jax Marlowe owes Vera Kade — five jobs stand between him and a clean slate. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome and play. No build step, no network: every file the game loads is under `game/`.

## How to play

- **W A S D** — walk / drive, **Shift** — run, **Space** — handbrake
- **E** — enter, steal, or exit a car
- **F** — punch, **Q** — pistol, **Click** — shoot
- **Drag mouse** — look around, **P** — pause, **R** — restart mission, **M** — mute

## What is in the city

- A full street grid with traffic, pedestrians, a marina with boats, a beach, a park, and a bank plaza — all original, nothing from any commercial game
- Five story missions: First Wheels, Package Run, Heat, The Pickup, and The Big One, with cash rewards and a save that keeps your progress
- A wanted level (0–5 stars) with police cars that chase, ram, and bust you; lay low and the heat fades
- Combat on foot, car stealing, a HUD with a live minimap, and synthesized sound and music (WebAudio, no audio files)
